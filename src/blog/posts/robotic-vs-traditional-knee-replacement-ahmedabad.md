    <!-- SECTION 2 -->
    <h2>Traditional Knee Replacement: What Works, What Doesn't</h2>

    <p style="line-height:1.8">Traditional knee replacement surgery has helped millions of patients across India and the world regain mobility. The surgeon uses calibrated instruments, anatomical landmarks, and hard-won experience to align and resurface the knee joint. It works. For many patients, it delivers excellent outcomes. But there are real trade-offs worth understanding before you decide.</p>

    <h3>The Traditional Approach — Strengths</h3>
    <ul style="margin:10px 0 18px 24px;line-height:1.9">
      <li><strong>Proven track record:</strong> 30+ years of reliable outcomes across millions of procedures globally</li>
      <li><strong>Widely available:</strong> Nearly every orthopedic surgeon in India is trained and experienced in traditional knee replacement</li>
      <li><strong>Effective for severe arthritis:</strong> Removes damaged bone and cartilage, restoring the joint surface reliably</li>
      <li><strong>Established recovery pathways:</strong> Physiotherapy centres everywhere understand traditional knee replacement recovery protocols</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>The Traditional Approach — The Hidden Costs</h3>
    <ul style="margin:10px 0 18px 24px;line-height:1.9">
      <li><strong>Manual precision limits:</strong> Even the most experienced surgeon's hands are subject to small variations — alignment can drift by 3–5mm, which may sound minor but affects long-term implant performance</li>
      <li><strong>Larger incisions and more tissue disruption:</strong> More soft tissue trauma means more swelling, more pain in the early weeks, and a slower path back to walking confidently</li>
      <li><strong>Extended hospital stay:</strong> Typically 3–4 days post-operatively, compared to 1–2 days with robotic-assisted surgery</li>
      <li><strong>Longer return to daily life:</strong> Most patients need 12–16 weeks before feeling fully confident on stairs, in cars, and walking outdoors</li>
      <li><strong>Higher long-term revision rates:</strong> Small misalignments accumulate over time — studies report revision surgery rates of 10–15% within 10 years for traditional knee replacement</li>
    </ul>

    <p style="line-height:1.8">Traditional surgery is not "bad" — it is just constrained by human precision. Robotic surgery removes that constraint.</p>

    <!-- SECTION 3 -->
    <h2>Robotic Knee Replacement: The Next-Generation Difference</h2>

    <p style="line-height:1.8">Robotic knee replacement is not about a robot doing the surgery on its own. It is about <strong>real-time, AI-guided precision</strong> that maps your unique anatomy during the procedure and executes the surgical plan to sub-millimetre accuracy — while your surgeon remains in complete control at every step.</p>

    <h3>How Dr. Hardik Shah's VELYS System Works (No Pre-Op CT Scans)</h3>

    <p style="line-height:1.8">Unlike older robotic systems that require radiation-heavy pre-operative CT scans — adding cost, delay, and radiation exposure to your journey — the 4th-generation <strong>VELYS Robotic System</strong> used by Dr. Hardik Shah at DHS Hospital is completely <strong>imageless</strong>. Here is what that means for you as a patient:</p>

    <ul style="margin:10px 0 18px 24px;line-height:1.9">
      <li><strong>Digital mapping happens during surgery:</strong> The robotic system scans and maps your knee anatomy in real-time on the operating table — zero pre-operative radiation exposure</li>
      <li><strong>Dynamic, patient-specific execution:</strong> The system continuously adjusts to your unique bone structure, alignment, and soft tissue as surgery progresses</li>
      <li><strong>Faster scheduling:</strong> No weeks spent waiting for CT imaging — once you are confirmed as a candidate, you move directly to surgery scheduling</li>
      <li><strong>Sub-millimetre precision:</strong> Bone cuts and implant positioning are accurate to within 0.5mm — a standard that human hands alone cannot consistently achieve</li>
      <li><strong>Minimal tissue damage:</strong> Smaller, more targeted incisions mean less bleeding, less swelling, and significantly less post-operative pain</li>
    </ul>
