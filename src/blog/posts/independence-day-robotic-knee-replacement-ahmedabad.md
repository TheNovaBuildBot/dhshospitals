    <p style="font-size:1.1rem;line-height:1.8">There's a quiet, personal kind of freedom that often gets overlooked as parents grow older: the freedom to climb a flight of stairs without wincing, to get up and greet the grandchildren without a hand on the armrest, or to stand through a full wedding ceremony without needing to sit down halfway through.</p>

    <p>Knee pain has a way of shrinking the world. A morning walk becomes a chore. A family function becomes an event to "manage" rather than enjoy. Slowly, without anyone quite noticing, independence gives way to hesitation. For many families in Ahmedabad, that's the moment they start asking whether it's time to look at knee replacement for a parent — and what that decision actually involves.</p>

    <h2>A New Kind of Precision: The VELYS Robotic-Assisted Solution</h2>

    <p>At DHS Multispecialty Hospital, Ahmedabad, we offer the <strong>VELYS Robotic-Assisted Solution</strong> by Johnson &amp; Johnson DePuy Synthes — one of the most advanced technologies available for knee replacement surgery today. What makes it particularly reassuring for families is this: it is completely <strong>imageless</strong>, meaning there's no need for a pre-operative CT scan. That's one less appointment, one less dose of radiation, and one less thing for an anxious senior parent to worry about before surgery.</p>

    <p>Instead, the system maps the knee in real time, inside the operating room, building a precise 3D picture of the patient's unique joint anatomy as the surgery happens. This lets the surgical team work with sub-millimetre precision, positioning the implant to match the knee's natural alignment far more accurately than conventional instruments allow.</p>

    <p>This programme is led by <strong>Dr. Hardik Shah</strong>, our Medical Director and orthopedic surgeon specialising in robotic joint replacement, who has performed over 2,000 knee replacement surgeries. He and his team focus not just on the technical success of the operation, but on what matters most to families — a safe procedure and a genuinely better quality of life afterward.</p>

    <h2>Recovery That Respects Family Time</h2>

    <p>One of the most common concerns families raise is recovery time. With robotic-assisted precision, many patients experience less tissue disruption during surgery, which often translates into a smoother, faster rehabilitation. Most patients walk the same day with support and go home within 2–3 days, with many walking unaided within 1–2 weeks. That means less time confined to bed, and more time getting back to what actually matters — a family dinner, a grandchild's school event, or simply moving through the day without pain setting the pace.</p>

    <p>Independence isn't tied to a date on the calendar. It's the ability to live life on your own terms, at any age — and for many seniors, a well-planned knee replacement is what makes that possible again.</p>

    <h2>Is It the Right Time to Consider Surgery?</h2>

    <p>Not every older parent with knee pain needs surgery immediately, and we never recommend it as a first step. A proper orthopedic evaluation — including X-rays and a clinical assessment — is the only reliable way to know whether physiotherapy, medication, or robotic knee replacement is the right path. Surgery is typically considered when X-rays show bone-on-bone wear, pain persists despite 6 or more months of conservative treatment, or daily activities like walking and climbing stairs have become consistently difficult.</p>
