    <h2>Why we brought robotic knee replacement to DHS</h2>
    <p>For decades, knee replacement in India was a manual procedure: a skilled surgeon, conventional jigs, and instinct refined over thousands of cases. Outcomes were good — but small alignment errors of 2°, 3°, or 4° were common, and over a 20-year horizon they translated into earlier wear, more revision surgery, and less natural-feeling knees.</p>
    <p>Robotic-assisted joint replacement closes that gap. The robot does not perform the surgery — the surgeon does. What the robot adds is a real-time, computer-verified plan and an instrument that cuts bone within a tightly controlled boundary. Published data on VELYS shows <strong>strong alignment accuracy compared with conventional manual technique</strong>.</p>

    <h2>What is the VELYS Robotic-Assisted Solution?</h2>
    <p>VELYS is a robotic platform developed by <strong>Johnson &amp; Johnson DePuy Synthes</strong>, designed for total knee replacement and paired with the J&amp;J Attune knee implant system. It is the <em>fastest-growing robotic knee platform in India since its November 2022 launch</em>, with thousands of cases performed across the country.</p>
    <p>What makes VELYS different from older platforms (Mako by Stryker, ROSA by Zimmer Biomet) is that it is <strong>imageless</strong>:</p>
    <ul style="margin:10px 0 18px 24px;line-height:1.9">
      <li><strong>No pre-operative CT scan.</strong> No extra radiation. No CT-scan cost or scheduling delay before surgery.</li>
      <li><strong>Intra-operative anatomy mapping.</strong> Specialised probes register your knee's bone landmarks and ligament tension during the procedure itself.</li>
      <li><strong>Real-time ligament balance.</strong> Software models how your knee will move once the implant is placed, and the surgeon refines the plan accordingly.</li>
      <li><strong>Sub-millimetre robotic accuracy.</strong> The robotic arm guides the bone cuts within tight tolerances that human hands alone cannot match.</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Who is a candidate for robotic knee replacement?</h2>
    <p>Robotic knee replacement is suited to most patients who would benefit from a conventional knee replacement — but particularly those who:</p>
    <ul style="margin:10px 0 18px 24px;line-height:1.9">
      <li>Have advanced osteoarthritis or rheumatoid arthritis with persistent pain and stiffness despite medication, physiotherapy, and injections.</li>
      <li>Have post-traumatic joint damage from an old injury that limits day-to-day activity.</li>
      <li>Prefer a more accurate, computer-verified surgical plan with potentially better long-term implant survival.</li>
      <li>Wish to avoid the pre-operative CT scan and extra radiation associated with older robotic platforms.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Whether you are the right candidate for robotic surgery, manual surgery, partial replacement, or non-surgical management is best decided in a face-to-face consultation. We do not push every patient toward the robot; we recommend it where it adds clinical value.</p>
