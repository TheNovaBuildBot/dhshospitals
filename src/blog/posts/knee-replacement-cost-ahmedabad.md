  <h2 style="color: #1a6fc4; font-size: 1.55rem; margin-top: 44px; border-left: 4px solid #1a6fc4; padding-left: 14px;">What Does a Knee Replacement Package Include?</h2>

  <p>When a hospital quotes you a price for knee replacement, that number should ideally cover a bundled set of services — but not every centre bundles the same things. Understanding what is <em>inside</em> the package protects you from surprise bills at discharge.</p>

  <p>A standard knee replacement package at a private hospital in Ahmedabad typically includes:</p>

  <ul style="line-height: 2.1; color: #333;">
    <li><strong>Surgeon's professional fee</strong> — operating orthopaedic surgeon and assistant</li>
    <li><strong>Operation Theatre (OT) charges</strong> — sterile environment, instruments, surgical team</li>
    <li><strong>Implant cost</strong> — the prosthetic knee joint (this is often the single largest variable)</li>
    <li><strong>Anaesthesia fee</strong> — spinal or general anaesthesia by a qualified anaesthesiologist</li>
    <li><strong>Hospital stay</strong> — room category you choose (general, semi-private, or private)</li>
    <li><strong>Physiotherapy sessions</strong> — typically 5–10 supervised sessions during admission</li>
    <li><strong>Consumables and nursing care</strong> — dressings, IV lines, medications during stay</li>
    <li><strong>Pre-operative investigations</strong> — blood work, ECG, chest X-ray (some packages include these, some bill separately)</li>
  </ul>

  <p>For <strong>conventional (non-robotic) knee replacement</strong>, most reputed private hospitals in Ahmedabad charge in the range of <strong>₹1,80,000 to ₹2,80,000</strong>. For <strong>robotic-assisted knee replacement</strong>, the range across Ahmedabad centres is typically <strong>₹2,50,000 to ₹3,80,000</strong> — though not all robotic systems carry the same hidden costs.</p>

  <div style="background: #e8f4fd; border-left: 4px solid #1a6fc4; border-radius: 6px; padding: 16px 20px; margin: 24px 0;">
    <strong>Important:</strong> Always ask for a written, itemised cost estimate before signing admission papers. Verbal quotes can differ significantly from the final bill.
  </div>

  <!-- SECTION 2 -->
  <h2 style="color: #1a6fc4; font-size: 1.55rem; margin-top: 44px; border-left: 4px solid #1a6fc4; padding-left: 14px;">Implant Choices and What They Cost</h2>

  <p>The implant — the artificial knee joint that replaces damaged cartilage and bone — is usually the <strong>single biggest variable in your total bill</strong>. Implant prices in India are regulated under the NPPA ceiling, but within that ceiling, quality and technology vary enormously.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #0a3d62; font-size: 1.15rem; margin-top: 28px;">Basic / Economy Implants — ₹30,000 to ₹50,000</h3>
  <p>Primarily Indian-manufactured, fixed-bearing implants. They meet regulatory standards and work adequately for lower-activity patients. However, they typically have shorter published longevity data and fewer size options, which can affect fit — especially for smaller or larger anatomy.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #0a3d62; font-size: 1.15rem; margin-top: 24px;">Mid-Range Implants — ₹60,000 to ₹90,000</h3>
  <p>Imported implants with fixed or mobile-bearing options. Brands in this range offer better size modularity and more published clinical data. A reasonable choice for most patients with standard anatomy and moderate activity goals.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #0a3d62; font-size: 1.15rem; margin-top: 24px;">Premium Implants — ₹90,000 to ₹1,40,000</h3>
  <p>The premium category includes globally recognised systems such as the <strong>Johnson &amp; Johnson ATTUNE</strong>, Zimmer Biomet Persona, and Smith &amp; Nephew Genesis II. These offer superior size graduation (millimetre-precise fits), advanced bearing surfaces for lower wear, and the longest independent clinical follow-up data — some extending 15–20 years.</p>

  <p>At DHS Multispecialty Hospital, <a href="/doctors/doctor-hardik/" style="color: #1a6fc4; font-weight: 600;">Dr. Hardik M Shah</a> preferentially pairs the <strong>J&amp;J ATTUNE implant system with the VELYS robotic platform</strong> — because VELYS is purpose-built and validated specifically for ATTUNE implants. This pairing means the robotic bone-cut planning is optimised for the implant that will actually go into your knee.</p>

  <p><strong>Implant selection is always a personalised conversation.</strong> Your age, activity level, anatomy, and budget all matter. Dr. Shah will walk you through options at consultation — never a pressured decision.</p>

  <!-- SECTION 3 -->
  <h2 style="color: #1a6fc4; font-size: 1.55rem; margin-top: 44px; border-left: 4px solid #1a6fc4; padding-left: 14px;">Hospital Stay and Room Costs</h2>

  <p>Room category is the second-largest variable in your knee replacement bill — and it is entirely within your control. Here is what room rates typically look like at private hospitals across Ahmedabad:</p>

  <ul style="line-height: 2.2; color: #333;">
    <li><strong>General Ward:</strong> ₹2,000 – ₹4,000 per night</li>
    <li><strong>Semi-Private Room (shared between 2 patients):</strong> ₹4,000 – ₹7,000 per night</li>
    <li><strong>Private Room (single occupancy):</strong> ₹7,000 – ₹15,000 per night</li>
  </ul>

  <p>A 5- to 7-night hospital stay adds <strong>₹35,000 to ₹1,05,000</strong> just in room charges — a significant and often underappreciated cost driver.</p>

  <p>At DHS, our <strong>standard discharge target is 48 hours</strong>. This is not about rushing recovery — it is built around evidence-based early mobilisation beginning on Day 1. Patients who stand and take assisted steps within hours of surgery recover faster, face lower blood clot risk, and have less reason to remain in hospital.</p>

  <p>The financial impact of a 2-night stay versus a 5-night stay at a private room rate is a saving of <strong>₹21,000 to ₹45,000</strong> — money that stays in your family's pocket without compromising clinical outcomes. In contemporary knee replacement literature, early discharge correlates with <em>better</em> outcomes, not worse.</p>

  <!-- SECTION 4 — KEY DIFFERENTIATOR -->
  <h2 style="color: #1a6fc4; font-size: 1.55rem; margin-top: 44px; border-left: 4px solid #1a6fc4; padding-left: 14px;">The Hidden Savings of VELYS Imageless Technology</h2>

  <p>This is the section most robotic knee replacement brochures won't show you — because it makes the comparison uncomfortable for hospitals using older robotic platforms.</p>

  <h3 style="color: #0a3d62; font-size: 1.15rem; margin-top: 28px;">Why Other Robots Charge You Extra Before Surgery Even Begins</h3>

  <p>Most robotic knee replacement systems currently available in India — including <strong>Stryker MAKO</strong> and <strong>CUVIS</strong> — are <em>CT-based</em>. They require a detailed CT scan of your knee before the surgical plan is uploaded into the robot. That pre-operative CT scan costs <strong>₹8,000 to ₹15,000</strong> and is almost always charged separately from the surgical package.</p>

  <p>Beyond the cost, a pre-operative CT scan means:</p>

  <ul style="line-height: 2.1; color: #333;">
    <li><strong>An extra hospital or diagnostic centre visit</strong> — inconvenient, especially for elderly patients with limited mobility</li>
    <li><strong>Radiation exposure</strong> — a CT scan delivers significantly more radiation than a standard X-ray</li>
    <li><strong>Planning delays of 1 to 2 weeks</strong> — the scan must be processed before your surgery date can be confirmed</li>
    <li><strong>Risk of plan changes</strong> — if your knee swelling shifts between the CT date and surgery day, the preoperative plan may no longer be fully accurate</li>
  </ul>

  <h3 style="color: #0a3d62; font-size: 1.15rem; margin-top: 24px;">How VELYS Imageless Technology Saves Money — and More</h3>

  <p>The <strong>VELYS Robotic-Assisted Solution by J&amp;J DePuy Synthes</strong> is <strong>100% imageless</strong> — it maps your unique knee anatomy <em>intraoperatively</em>, in real time, on the day of surgery. No pre-operative CT scan. No radiation. No extra appointment. No wait.</p>

  <p>For patients at DHS Multispecialty Hospital, this translates directly into:</p>

  <ul style="line-height: 2.1; color: #333;">
    <li><strong>₹0 CT scan cost</strong> — saving ₹8,000 to ₹15,000 immediately</li>
    <li><strong>Zero radiation exposure</strong> from pre-operative imaging</li>
    <li><strong>Surgery scheduled in days, not weeks</strong> — no waiting for scan processing</li>
    <li><strong>Anatomy mapping that reflects your knee as it is on surgery day</strong> — not as it was weeks before</li>
