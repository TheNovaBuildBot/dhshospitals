  <h1 itemprop="headline" style="color:#0a3d62; font-size:2rem; font-weight:800; line-height:1.3; margin-bottom:22px;">
    Walking Within 24 Hours: The Real Recovery Timeline of Robotic Knee Replacement Patients
  </h1>

  <p style="font-size:1.05rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8; margin-bottom:14px;">
    Not long ago, a knee replacement meant resigning yourself to a week or more in a hospital bed, weeks of severe post-operative pain, and a slow, gruelling return to normal life that could stretch across many months. Patients were told to clear their calendars for the better part of a year before expecting anything close to comfortable, independent movement.
  </p>
  <p style="font-size:1.05rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8; margin-bottom:14px;">
    That story has fundamentally changed. At DHS Multispecialty Hospital, Ahmedabad, patients undergoing robotic knee replacement with the <strong style="color:#0a3d62;">VELYS&trade; Robotic-Assisted Solution</strong> are standing with support on the same day of surgery, taking their first supervised steps within 24 hours, and returning home in as little as 48 hours. This is not marketing language — it is the documented, reproducible clinical reality of precision robotic surgery. This article walks you through every milestone.
  </p>

  <h2 style="color:#1a6fc4; font-size:1.55rem; margin-top:44px; border-left:4px solid #1a6fc4; padding-left:14px;">
    Why VELYS Enables Faster Recovery: The Science of Soft-Tissue Protection
  </h2>

  <p style="font-size:1rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8; margin-bottom:14px;">
    Speed of recovery after knee replacement is not determined by willpower or pain tolerance — it is determined by how much trauma the surrounding soft tissue experiences during surgery. Conventional total knee replacement involves significant disruption to muscles, ligaments, and connective tissue simply to achieve adequate visualisation and manual bone-cutting accuracy.
  </p>
  <p style="font-size:1rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8; margin-bottom:22px;">
    More tissue trauma means more inflammation, more swelling, more pain, and a longer healing cascade before rehabilitation can meaningfully begin. The VELYS robotic system is engineered, at every stage, to minimise that trauma. Here is exactly how.
  </p>

  <div style="background:#f4f9ff; border-left:4px solid #1a6fc4; border-radius:0 8px 8px 0; padding:16px 20px; margin-bottom:18px;">
    <p style="margin:0 0 6px 0; font-weight:700; color:#0a3d62; font-size:0.97rem;">● VELYS Advantage 1: Imageless Real-Time Intraoperative Mapping</p>
    <p style="margin:0; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.75;">VELYS is 100% imageless — unlike CT-scan-based robotic systems, it maps your knee anatomy dynamically during the procedure itself using real-time intraoperative sensors. There is no pre-operative CT scan, no radiation exposure, and no gap between the map and the actual joint being operated on. The system is always working with live, current anatomy — not a weeks-old static scan.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="background:#f4f9ff; border-left:4px solid #1a6fc4; border-radius:0 8px 8px 0; padding:16px 20px; margin-bottom:18px;">
    <p style="margin:0 0 6px 0; font-weight:700; color:#0a3d62; font-size:0.97rem;">● VELYS Advantage 2: Sub-Millimetre Bone Cut Precision</p>
    <p style="margin:0; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.75;">VELYS constrains the cutting instrument robotically, executing bone cuts within 0.5&nbsp;mm of the planned target. Only the tissue that must be removed is removed — surrounding bone, cartilage, and soft tissue is disturbed as little as physically possible, dramatically reducing the post-operative inflammatory response and pain levels.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="background:#f4f9ff; border-left:4px solid #1a6fc4; border-radius:0 8px 8px 0; padding:16px 20px; margin-bottom:18px;">
    <p style="margin:0 0 6px 0; font-weight:700; color:#0a3d62; font-size:0.97rem;">● VELYS Advantage 3: Reduced Blood Loss and Post-Operative Swelling</p>
    <p style="margin:0; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.75;">Greater precision translates directly into fewer inadvertent vessel disruptions and less widespread tissue damage, resulting in measurably lower intraoperative blood loss. Less blood loss means less reactive swelling in the first 48–72 hours — the window that most determines how soon a patient can safely bear weight and begin walking.</p>
  </div>

  <div style="background:#f4f9ff; border-left:4px solid #1a6fc4; border-radius:0 8px 8px 0; padding:16px 20px; margin-bottom:28px;">
    <p style="margin:0 0 6px 0; font-weight:700; color:#0a3d62; font-size:0.97rem;">● VELYS Advantage 4: Superior Implant Positioning for Natural Movement</p>
    <p style="margin:0; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.75;">A perfectly positioned implant replicates the patient's natural biomechanics from day one. Muscles do not need to compensate for poor mechanics — they can resume their normal function earlier. This biomechanical harmony is a core driver of faster physiotherapy progress, earlier discharge, and a shorter overall rehabilitation arc.</p>
  </div>

The timeline below reflects the typical experience of Dr. Hardik Shah's VELYS patients at DHS Ahmedabad.

          <li><strong>Outpatient Physiotherapy:</strong> Formal physiotherapy sessions begin — typically 3 times per week at a local physiotherapy centre</li>
        </ul>
        <div style="background:#f0f7ff; border-radius:6px; padding:10px 14px; margin-top:14px; font-size:0.88rem; color:#0a3d62;"><strong>🎯 Week 2 Goal:</strong> Walking 100+ metres with walker. Knee bend 90°. Swelling substantially reduced. Off most prescription pain medication.</div>

    <ul style="margin:0; padding-left:20px; color:#333; font-size:0.97rem; line-height:2.2;">
      <li>🚿 <strong>Bathroom grab bars</strong> — install next to toilet and shower/bath before surgery</li>
      <li>🪑 <strong>Raised toilet seat adapter</strong> — reduces the depth of the sitting bend; available at medical supply stores</li>
      <li>🟫 <strong>Remove loose rugs and carpets</strong> — major trip hazard for walker users; secure or remove entirely</li>
      <li>🛏 <strong>Ground-floor sleeping arrangement</strong> — avoid stairs for the first 5–7 days if possible</li>
      <li>💊 <strong>Stock medications</strong> — fill prescriptions before admission so medicines are ready at home on discharge</li>
      <li>👨‍👩‍👧 <strong>Designate a family caregiver</strong> for the first two weeks — someone who can assist with bathing, cooking, and transport</li>

  <h2 style="color:#1a6fc4; font-size:1.55rem; margin-top:44px; border-left:4px solid #1a6fc4; padding-left:14px;">
    Frequently Asked Questions
  </h2>

  <details style="border:1px solid #d0e3f7; border-radius:6px; margin-bottom:14px; overflow:hidden;">
    <summary style="background:#f8fbff; padding:15px 20px; cursor:pointer; font-weight:600; color:#0a3d62; list-style:none;">Will I need a caregiver at home after discharge?</summary>
    <div style="padding:16px 22px; background:#fff; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8;">
      Yes — for the first two weeks after discharge, a designated family caregiver is strongly recommended. You will be mobile and independent for basic needs (walking, sitting, eating), but you will need assistance with bathing, stair navigation for the first few days, and transport to your Day 7 follow-up visit. From Week 3 onwards, most patients manage independently.
    </div>
  </details>

  <details style="border:1px solid #d0e3f7; border-radius:6px; margin-bottom:14px; overflow:hidden;">
    <summary style="background:#f8fbff; padding:15px 20px; cursor:pointer; font-weight:600; color:#0a3d62; list-style:none;">When can I climb stairs after robotic knee replacement?</summary>
    <div style="padding:16px 22px; background:#fff; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8;">
      Stair practice begins in the physiotherapy sessions during Week 3–4. Most VELYS patients at DHS are managing stairs comfortably with a handrail by the end of Week 4. By Week 6, the majority can handle stairs without holding the rail for short flights. Individual progress varies based on age, fitness level, and physiotherapy adherence.
    </div>
  </details>

  <details style="border:1px solid #d0e3f7; border-radius:6px; margin-bottom:14px; overflow:hidden;">
    <summary style="background:#f8fbff; padding:15px 20px; cursor:pointer; font-weight:600; color:#0a3d62; list-style:none;">Is physiotherapy painful after VELYS surgery?</summary>
    <div style="padding:16px 22px; background:#fff; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8;">
      Physiotherapy after VELYS robotic knee replacement is significantly less painful than after conventional surgery, primarily due to reduced soft-tissue trauma. Discomfort during exercises — particularly when increasing bend range — is expected and normal. Sharp or severe pain is not, and should be reported immediately. DHS physiotherapists work within your comfort tolerance and progress the programme at your pace.
    </div>
  </details>

  <details style="border:1px solid #d0e3f7; border-radius:6px; margin-bottom:36px; overflow:hidden;">
    <summary style="background:#f8fbff; padding:15px 20px; cursor:pointer; font-weight:600; color:#0a3d62; list-style:none;">When can I travel by car or flight after knee replacement?</summary>
    <div style="padding:16px 22px; background:#fff; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8;">
      Short car journeys (under 30 minutes) are generally permitted from Week 2 onwards, with the seat adjusted for knee comfort. For longer road or air travel, Week 4–6 is the typical window — with the strong recommendation to take walk-and-stretch breaks every 45–60 minutes to prevent deep vein thrombosis. Your specific travel clearance will be confirmed at your follow-up consultations with Dr. Shah.
    </div>
  </details>
