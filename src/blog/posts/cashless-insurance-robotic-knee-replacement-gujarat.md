  <h2 style="color:#1a6fc4; font-size:1.55rem; margin-top:44px; border-left:4px solid #1a6fc4; padding-left:14px; font-weight:700;">
    Is Robotic (VELYS) Knee Replacement Covered by Insurance? The Myth vs Reality
  </h2>

  <p style="font-size:1rem; color:#333; line-height:1.75; margin-bottom:14px;">
    One of the most common concerns we hear from patients in Ahmedabad and across Gujarat is: <em>"Doctor, will my insurance company reject the claim because the surgery is robotic?"</em> This fear — while understandable — is largely a myth, and it stops many patients from accessing better surgical technology that they are fully entitled to claim.
  </p>

  <p style="font-size:1rem; color:#333; line-height:1.75; margin-bottom:14px;">
    The reality is that <strong>VELYS Robotic-Assisted Surgery</strong> is billed under the standard procedure code for <em>"robotic-assisted knee arthroplasty"</em> — a category recognised by all major insurers in India. The robotic system is a surgical tool that improves precision; it does not reclassify the procedure into an "experimental" or "exclusion" category.
  </p>

  <p style="font-size:1rem; color:#333; line-height:1.75; margin-bottom:20px;">
    Most comprehensive health insurance policies with a <strong>sum insured of ₹5 lakh or above</strong> cover robotic knee replacement — provided the hospital is empanelled and the procedure is pre-authorised. Crucially, the DHS billing team verifies your exact coverage and confirms inclusion of robotic-assisted surgery <strong>before your surgery date is ever scheduled</strong>.
  </p>

    Frequently Asked Questions

  <details style="border:1px solid #d0e3f7; border-radius:6px; margin-bottom:14px; overflow:hidden;">
    <summary style="background:#f8fbff; padding:15px 20px; cursor:pointer; font-weight:600; color:#0a3d62; list-style:none;">Can I use two insurance policies for knee replacement at DHS?</summary>
    <div style="padding:16px 22px; background:#fff; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8;">
      Yes — this is known as co-insurance or coordination of benefits. If your primary policy has a sum-insured sub-limit for implants or the approved amount falls short, your secondary policy (if held with a different insurer) may be used to cover the shortfall. Our billing team will coordinate both claims simultaneously, ensuring you maximise your legitimate insurance entitlement.
    </div>
  </details>

    <div style="padding:16px 22px; background:#fff; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8;">
      Rejections are rare at DHS because we verify coverage before submitting. If a rejection does occur, our billing team immediately assists with the appeals process — submitting additional clinical justification from Dr. Hardik Shah, requesting a review from the insurer's medical officer. In parallel, we present you with a clear self-pay package so you can make an informed decision without delays to your surgery.
    </div>
  </details>

  <details style="border:1px solid #d0e3f7; border-radius:6px; margin-bottom:14px; overflow:hidden;">
    <summary style="background:#f8fbff; padding:15px 20px; cursor:pointer; font-weight:600; color:#0a3d62; list-style:none;">Does robotic knee replacement require a specific waiting period under my policy?</summary>
    <div style="padding:16px 22px; background:#fff; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8;">
      Knee replacement surgeries may fall under the pre-existing disease (PED) waiting period of your policy — typically 2–4 years from policy inception. Robotic-assisted surgery itself does not carry an additional waiting period; it is the underlying condition (osteoarthritis/knee degeneration) that determines eligibility, not the technology used. Our billing team checks your specific PED waiting period status when you first present at OPD.
    </div>
  </details>

  <details style="border:1px solid #d0e3f7; border-radius:6px; margin-bottom:36px; overflow:hidden;">
    <summary style="background:#f8fbff; padding:15px 20px; cursor:pointer; font-weight:600; color:#0a3d62; list-style:none;">What is a TPA and how does DHS work with them?</summary>
    <div style="padding:16px 22px; background:#fff; font-size:0.95rem; color:#333; line-height:1.8;">
      A Third-Party Administrator (TPA) is a company licensed by IRDAI that processes cashless and reimbursement claims on behalf of insurance companies. Most major insurers outsource their hospital empanelment and claim processing to a TPA. DHS is registered with the TPAs of all major insurers, and our billing desk has established direct contacts with each — significantly reducing response time on your pre-authorization compared to patients approaching TPAs independently.
    </div>
